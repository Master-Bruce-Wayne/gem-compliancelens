## 1. Site Vision
GemOne ComplianceLens is an AI-powered procurement compliance SaaS platform. The marketing site uses a warm, editorial, human aesthetic — Dovetail-inspired with oversized confident typography, cream backgrounds, SVG bezier section transitions, and pill-shaped CTAs in deep ink-indigo.

## 2. Design System
See `../docs/DESIGN.md` for full spec.
Key tokens: cream #FDF3E4, ink #2A1B54, inkMuted #4C3B7C, inkDark #1F1440, indigoDark #241454, plus teal/coral/pink/sky accents.
Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans (headings, 800-900wt) + Inter (body, 400-500wt).
Buttons: pill-shaped (border-radius 999px), solid ink fill, white text.
Cards: rounded-2xl (20px), white, soft ambient shadow.
Section transitions: SVG bezier wave dividers.
